# Aloom Product Context

## Product

Aloom is a self-hosted GEO detection and recurring monitoring tool. It measures how an existing product appears in answers collected from the official Web interfaces of Doubao, DeepSeek, Yuanbao, and Qwen. It uses fixed, versioned prompt suites, persistent local browser profiles, sample-level checkpoints, structured analysis, and comparable provider reports.

## Users

- Product marketing, SEO, and GEO teams measuring AI answer visibility.
- Agencies monitoring multiple client products.
- Technical self-hosters who need auditable official-Web collection instead of API substitutions.

## Core Jobs

1. Confirm a product profile and its competitors.
2. Select a fixed detection suite, language, providers, modes, and sampling depth.
3. Collect answers and visible citations from official AI Web interfaces.
4. Compare visibility, recommendation, evidence, factuality, competition, stability, and completion by provider and over time.

## Brand Personality

Precise, restrained, credible, and operational. Aloom should feel like a measurement instrument rather than a promotional content tool.

## Visual Direction

- Near-black and white form the base palette; signal cyan is a controlled accent.
- The `AL` monogram is the primary graphic mark. Display the Aloom name once in a header hierarchy.
- Prefer quiet, information-dense layouts with clear status, evidence, and uncertainty.
- Avoid green-dominant palettes, decorative gradients, ornamental effects, oversized marketing copy, and repeated brand naming.

## Accessibility

- Target WCAG AA contrast for text and interactive controls.
- Do not communicate status or score solely through color.
- Preserve readable type, visible focus, keyboard access, and reduced-motion preferences.

## Design Principles

1. One clear identity signal per surface.
2. Evidence before claims.
3. Completion and uncertainty remain visible.
4. Provider differences are easy to compare.
5. Brand accents support the data instead of competing with it.
